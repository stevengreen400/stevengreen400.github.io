Core Focus

B2B & Brand Marketing, Consumer Behavior, Market Research & Financial ROI

What drew me to marketing in the first place is simple: I like figuring out why people and businesses choose the brands they do. Good marketing isn't just about putting a product in front of someone, it's about understanding how decisionmakers think, telling a story that actually resonates, and building trust over time.

At the same time, adding a minor in finance changed how I look at creative work. A great campaign idea is only half the picture; it also has to make sense on a spreadsheet. Studying finance gave me the analytical foundation to look at marketing through the lens of unit economics, cash flow, and real return on investment. I enjoy sitting right at that intersection, where brand and B2B strategy meet business reality.

Outside of the classroom at Purdue, I stay deeply involved in campus leadership. As the VP of Fundraising and Community Service on the Executive Board for the Society of Minority Managers (SMM), I lead fundraising initiatives, partner outreach, and service projects. Alongside my role as a Resident Assistant, these experiences have taught me how to manage people, communicate clearly, and stay level-headed when solving unexpected problems.
